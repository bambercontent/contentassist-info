contentassist

contentassist is a small desktop application that its owner runs on their own computer. It is not a public service and has no other users. This website is only an information page for the application.

Legal pages: Terms of Service · Privacy Policy

Intended purpose

The owner intends to use it as a private research tool, to review public TikTok content for creative ideas: sounds, hashtags, subjects and conversations, and video formats. That research is not implemented. The application cannot currently access any other creator's content, any recommendation feed, or any sound or trend data, and it does not claim to.

What works today

  • Signing in with one TikTok account that the owner controls, through TikTok Login Kit for Desktop, and approving the permissions on TikTok's own consent page.
  • Reading that account's basic profile (permission user.info.basic).
  • Requesting that account's own public video list (permission video.list).
  • Encrypted local storage of the application's credentials, and a command that deletes them.

Signing in only shows that the connection to that one account works. It does not give access to recommendations or to other people's videos.

Status

In development, in TikTok's sandbox only. The application has not been submitted to TikTok for review and has not been approved by TikTok.

What it does not do

  • It does not post, edit, delete or message anything on TikTok.
  • It does not read any account other than the one the owner signs in with.
  • It does not collect recommendations, trends, hashtags, sounds or other creators' videos.
  • This website does not collect information from visitors.
